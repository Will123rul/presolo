Before you rely on any of this

These are study aids, not an authority. The POH for your tail number, the current FARs, and your CFI outrank anything written here, in that order. Regulation citations are included so you can look them up yourself — and you should. Aircraft-specific numbers shown as examples are for a Cessna 172S; replace them with yours.
